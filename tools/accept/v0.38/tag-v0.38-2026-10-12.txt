== The deploy
dist/ is git-ignored and not committed, there is no .github/ workflow, and Cloudflare is git-connected to
github.com/tbkraf08/RetinaRave and builds on a push to main (wrangler.jsonc serves ./dist). The push IS the deploy:
ONE push, then poll https://retinarave.com/ for the VER line — never a second push (§115). The push and LIVE times
are in DECISIONS §125.
